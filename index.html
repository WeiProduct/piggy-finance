Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Piggy and personal finance management.

Is my financial data secure with Piggy?

Absolutely! Piggy uses bank-level 256-bit encryption to protect your data. We never store your bank credentials, and all information is transmitted using secure connections. Your privacy and security are our top priorities.

How much does Piggy cost?

Piggy offers a free basic plan that includes expense tracking, budgeting, and basic reports. Our Premium plan ($4.99/month) unlocks advanced features like investment tracking, goal planning, and personalized financial insights.

Can I connect multiple bank accounts?

Yes! Piggy supports connections to over 10,000 financial institutions worldwide. You can connect multiple bank accounts, credit cards, investment accounts, and loans to get a complete picture of your finances.

How do I get started with Piggy?

Getting started is easy! Download the app from your device's app store, create an account, and follow the guided setup process. You can be up and running in less than 5 minutes.
