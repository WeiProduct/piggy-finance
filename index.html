    Piggy Accounting is free to download on the App Store.

    Record expenses and income by category, manage bills, set a monthly budget, and review clear statistics.

    How does the AI financial analysis work?

    The app turns your recorded transactions into a plain-language analysis of income, savings, and spending, along with practical advice.

    Spreadsheets work, but they live on your laptop. Piggy Accounting is in your pocket: logging takes a couple of taps, charts build themselves, and AI analysis explains the numbers in plain language.
